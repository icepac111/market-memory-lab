# Research Topic Discovery Framework

Market Memory Lab is intended to help identify and evaluate a strong
FinTech PhD research direction within approximately one year.

## Candidate-topic record

Each candidate topic must include:

- topic identifier
- working title
- precise research question
- proposed contribution
- closest known literature
- unresolved gap
- falsifiable hypotheses
- required datasets
- data-access constraints
- mathematical prerequisites
- computational requirements
- proposed baselines
- proposed null models
- validation design
- replication plan
- ethical and licensing constraints
- strongest supporting evidence
- strongest contradicting evidence
- reasons the topic may fail
- next decisive experiment

## Evaluation dimensions

Candidate topics will be evaluated on:

1. Scientific importance
2. Novelty
3. Mathematical depth
4. Falsifiability
5. Data feasibility
6. Reproducibility
7. Robustness to alternative explanations
8. Relevance to FinTech practice
9. Potential for multiple coherent studies
10. Potential value to researchers and practitioners

## Decision discipline

No topic will be selected solely because:

- it produces attractive charts
- it uses deep learning
- it reports a high accuracy value
- it produces a positive backtest
- it matches a preferred narrative
- it appears fashionable
- one estimator supports it

The selected topic should survive direct attempts to disprove it.
