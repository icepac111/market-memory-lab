# Claim Boundaries

## Supported

- The two series can be aligned on 5,932 common valid observations.
- Their levels and successive changes are positively correlated in this sample.
- The rolling change relationship varies through time.
- The nominal-real difference was 2.33 percentage points on the latest
  common date in the frozen sample.

## Not supported

- Exact expected inflation
- Inflation forecasting
- Return forecasting
- Causal attribution
- Trading profitability
- Investment recommendations
- A universal structural law
